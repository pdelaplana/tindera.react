# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice
- **Professional & Empathetic:** Our tone is supportive, reliable, and respectful of the merchant's time and business challenges. We use clear, direct language to help users manage their livelihood with confidence.

## Visual Identity
- **Clarity & High Contrast:** Readability is non-negotiable, especially for high-glare environments (e.g., outdoor market stalls). We prioritize clear typography and high contrast for users of all ages and conditions.
- **Mobile-First Interactions:** Designed for speed and one-handed operation. Large tap targets and intuitive gestures are the foundation of our interface.
- **Visual Hierarchy & Semantic Cues:** We use distinct colors and purposeful iconography to guide merchants through tasks at a glance. Success and error states are visually unambiguous to ensure errors are caught quickly.
