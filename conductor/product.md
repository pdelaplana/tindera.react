# Initial Concept
An Ionic project called tindera.react, seemingly a POS and inventory management system.

# Product Definition

## Target Users
- **Small to medium-sized retail merchants and shop owners:** Business owners who need a streamlined way to manage their daily sales and inventory without complex, enterprise-level overhead.

## Goals
- **Simplify Operations:** Streamline sales recording and inventory tracking for daily business activities.
- **Drive Growth:** Provide deep financial analytics and reporting to help merchants make data-driven decisions.
- **Resilient Connectivity:** Enable offline-first capabilities to ensure business continuity even in areas with unstable network connections.

## Core Features
- **Point of Sale (POS):** A comprehensive checkout system including transaction recording, voiding, refunds, and flexible discount handling.
- **Inventory Management:** Robust tracking with support for multiple units of measure (UOM), package sizes, and automated low-stock alerts.
- **Multi-user Support:** Secure role-based access control to distinguish between Shop Owners (admin access) and Staff (operational access).
